Overview

Tiny Key Magic is an offline animation app designed for children to enjoy with adult supervision. It does not require an account, and is designed without advertising, third-party analytics, or cloud services.

What the app collects

In the current app design, Tiny Key Magic does not collect or transmit personally identifying information, typed content, device identifiers, location, contacts, photographs or usage analytics to the developer or third parties.

Keyboard and touch input: The mobile app responds to taps and keyboard input while open. The Mac desktop version may ask for macOS Input Monitoring or Accessibility access in order to detect its parent-controlled keyboard shortcut. Input is used locally to trigger effects and is not intended to be recorded, stored or sent from the device.

Local preferences: Desktop settings such as session duration, sound preferences and automatic-start options may be saved on your device to remember your choices. These preferences are not uploaded to a server.

Children's privacy

Tiny Key Magic does not provide public profiles, messaging, chat, advertising or account registration. It is intended for supervised play. Parents can end a session using the in-app or desktop parent controls.

Third parties and purchases

App Store and Google Play purchases and downloads are handled by the respective store operator under its own privacy terms. Tiny Key Magic does not integrate a separate in-app payment processor or advertising network.

Retention and deletion

Because the application is designed not to collect personal data on a developer-operated server, no server-side account records are maintained by the app. Local preferences may be removed by uninstalling the app or by the operating system's applicable application-data controls. Contact support for questions about these practices.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. Material changes will be described here, with an updated effective date.
